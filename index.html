Our Story

Designed Around Your Escape

Velmora began in 2011 as a six-room guesthouse on the Mirissa shoreline, built on one simple idea: the best escapes are the ones that feel personal.

Today we welcome travellers from more than sixty countries to two coastal properties on Sri Lanka's southern coast. We have stayed deliberately small. Fewer rooms mean our team knows every guest by name, our kitchens cook with what the local fishermen and farmers bring in each morning, and every space is designed to be quiet.

Our architecture draws on Geoffrey Bawa's tropical modernism: open pavilions, local stone and timber, and courtyards that let the sea breeze through.
